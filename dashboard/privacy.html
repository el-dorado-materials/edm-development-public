Dashboard Privacy Notice

Version prepared October 28, 2026. Effective upon publication.

About this notice

El Dorado Materials, LLC (EDM) operates the El Dorado Materials Dashboard for authorized business use. This notice explains how the dashboard handles sign-in information, QuickBooks connection information and records accessed for authorized testing and reporting. EDM determines the purposes of this processing; the dashboard is not operated on Intuit's behalf. Contact: connor@eldoradomaterials.com.

Current scope

The currently deployed QuickBooks connection supports only a designated sample company. Connecting EDM's real company requires separate production setup and the owner's authorization. The first authorized live test is limited to a small sample of accounting time records. That test does not authorize accounting edits, payroll processing, payment processing or scheduled synchronization. Expanding the dashboard's access or purposes requires a reviewed implementation and updated disclosures where appropriate.

Information we handle

The dashboard processes the authorized user's verified email address and sign-in status; the connected company identifier; OAuth access and refresh tokens; connection status, expiration and security state; and technical information needed to operate and protect the service.

For the first authorized live test, time records may include employee or vendor references and names, customer or job references, work dates, duration and work descriptions. QuickBooks may include additional fields in its response, including rates; the comparison view is limited to the fields needed for the approved test. These records do not by themselves establish wages or fully loaded labor costs.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate authorized users, establish and manage the approved QuickBooks connection, check the availability and accuracy of data needed for EDM's reporting, investigate failures and protect access. We use QuickBooks information only for the authorized functionality and within the company's authorization. We do not sell or rent QuickBooks information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Providers, sharing and locations

Intuit provides QuickBooks authorization and API services. Cloudflare provides application hosting, access controls and database services. These providers process information needed to supply their services under their applicable terms. Cloud infrastructure may process information in the United States and other locations; this notice does not represent that all processing is confined to the United States.

The initial release restricts application access to the designated EDM owner. The limited live test does not send returned business records to an AI model or other business-system integrations. Other data flows or additional user access require review and appropriate disclosures before activation. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to address unauthorized use or protect legal rights.

Storage and retention

Connection tokens are encrypted in application storage using an encryption secret kept separately from the stored token data. Connection information is retained while needed for the authorized connection, including resolving a failed revocation or recovering access. A successful disconnect revokes the grant and clears the active stored connection tokens; a failed revocation is reported rather than treated as success.

The first live test is designed to process returned business records in request memory and an authenticated result view, without retaining those returned records in the application database or sending them to application analytics. Application diagnostics are limited to information needed for troubleshooting and must exclude credentials, tokens, authorization codes and raw business-record payloads. Infrastructure providers may retain technical service and security information under their policies.

Cloudflare D1 maintains recovery history according to the service plan: its published recovery windows are currently seven days on Workers Free and thirty days on Workers Paid. Clearing an active record does not immediately remove provider recovery copies, and a recovery window is not a guarantee of final physical erasure. Requests affecting backups are handled through the provider's applicable retention and deletion processes. Required business records and copies independently saved by authorized users are handled separately. Disconnecting the dashboard does not delete source records in QuickBooks.

Security and cookies

We use HTTPS, owner-restricted access and multi-factor authentication for dashboard access. Authentication uses cookies and temporary security state to maintain sign-in and protect the authorization flow. The application does not use advertising cookies. Security measures reduce risk but do not guarantee that all failures or unauthorized access can be prevented. Keep devices, sign-in sessions, authenticators and recovery information protected.

Your choices and requests

You may use the connection page to disconnect an available connection or use the applicable Intuit controls to revoke the app's authorization. Contact connor@eldoradomaterials.com for access, correction, deletion, retention or privacy questions. We will verify authority before acting on requests and handle them subject to applicable law and required recordkeeping. Do not include passwords, connection tokens, authorization codes or unnecessary employee records in email.

Changes

We will update this notice before materially changing the dashboard's data collection, purposes, sharing or retention, and seek additional authorization where required. The version date identifies the published text. Review updated notices before enabling expanded features.
