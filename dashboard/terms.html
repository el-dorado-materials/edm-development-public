Private business dashboard

Dashboard User Agreement

Version prepared October 28, 2026. Effective upon publication.

Purpose and permission to use

El Dorado Materials, LLC (EDM) provides this private dashboard for authorized EDM business use. EDM grants authorized users limited, nontransferable permission to access the functions assigned to their accounts. This permission ends when access is withdrawn or the applicable authorization ends. This agreement does not transfer ownership of the dashboard or third-party services.

Current and initial live functionality

The currently deployed QuickBooks connection supports only a designated sample company. Production-company access requires separate setup and the owner's authorization. The first authorized live test is limited to up to five accounting time records for the owner-approved test date. The initial test performs business-data reads only; it does not edit QuickBooks records, run payroll, process payments or enable scheduled synchronization. Additional reporting functions require separately defined and authorized access.

Accounts and authorization

Use only an account authorized by EDM. Keep credentials, authenticator enrollment information, recovery information and sessions private. Do not share an account, bypass access controls, connect an unauthorized company or use the dashboard to obtain information outside your assigned authority. Access to EDM does not grant access to another company's QuickBooks records. Report suspected unauthorized access promptly to connor@eldoradomaterials.com.

QuickBooks connection

Review the application and company shown on Intuit's consent screen before granting access. Intuit's Accounting OAuth permission is broader than read-only; the dashboard's implemented operations restrict the initial test to the approved reads. This agreement does not expand that authorization. Intuit and other providers supply their services under their own applicable terms. EDM's dashboard is not operated by Intuit and is not an Intuit App Store listing.

Results and business decisions

Check results against the authoritative QuickBooks or Time records before relying on them. A missing or empty result does not establish that an employee did not work. Time entries, billable rates and zero cost fields do not establish wages, overtime treatment, payroll readiness or fully loaded labor expense. The first test cannot approve payroll, authorize financial changes, establish customer pricing or certify compliance. Data availability and accuracy depend in part on source records, permissions, connectivity and provider services.

Privacy and acceptable use

The accompanying Privacy Notice explains information handling and forms part of the conditions for using the dashboard. Use business and employee information only for authorized purposes, protect any copies you make and comply with applicable law and provider terms. Do not interfere with the service, attempt unauthorized access or disclose another person's or company's information without authority.

Availability and support

The dashboard may be unavailable or limited during maintenance, provider outages, connection expiry or an investigation. EDM may suspend access to protect the service or address misuse. No uninterrupted availability or error-free result is promised. Nothing in this agreement excludes rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded. For support, privacy requests or inaccurate results, contact connor@eldoradomaterials.com. Do not send passwords, connection tokens, authorization codes or unnecessary employee records by email.

Disconnecting and termination

Use the available disconnect control or applicable Intuit controls to revoke the app's authorization. A successful dashboard disconnect revokes the connection and clears active stored connection tokens; failures must be resolved rather than assumed successful. Disconnection does not erase source QuickBooks records. Application and provider retention are described in the Privacy Notice. EDM may end or restrict access when authority is withdrawn or use conflicts with this agreement.

Changes

Future features are not supplied merely because they appear in a development plan. Material changes to functions, permissions or data handling require appropriate review, authorization and updated disclosures. EDM may update this agreement and will identify the published version date. Users must review changed conditions before using newly enabled functions.
